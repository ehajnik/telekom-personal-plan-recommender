# Consumer usage archetypes (reference)

| Archetype | Typical data | Voice | SMS | Roaming | Notes |
|-----------|-------------|-------|-----|---------|-------|
| **Streamer** | High (60–150 GB) | Low–medium | Low | Low–medium | Video, social, home Wi‑Fi offloading |
| **Chatterbox** | Low–medium | High (800+ min) | Medium–high | Low | Voice-centric, moderate messaging |
| **Essential** | Low (<15 GB) | Low–medium | Low | Rare | Cost-sensitive, basic smartphone use |
| **Roamer** | Medium | Medium | Low | High (10+ days/mo) | Cross-border travel, EU/non-EU mix |
| **Messenger** | Medium | Low | High (100+) | Low | SMS/WhatsApp-heavy, moderate data |

**Overlays (may combine with any archetype):**
- **Data growth** — positive data trend; candidate for higher data tier or boosters
- **Voice decline** — negative voice trend; legacy voice bundle may be oversized
- **Roaming-heavy** — high roaming days vs low domestic roaming need in archetype
