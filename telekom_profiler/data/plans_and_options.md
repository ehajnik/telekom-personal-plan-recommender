## Selection hints (PoC)

- **Size by data:** pick the cheapest tariff whose allowance covers current usage × (1 + positive annual trend) × 1.1 headroom.
- **Light / occasional** → Prepaid S (≤ 1 GB and ≤ 50 off-net minutes) or Prepaid M/L; contract alternative MagentaMobil XS.
- **Streaming & data-heavy** → MagentaMobil L (≤ ~90 GB) or XL (unlimited); Prepaid XL (80 GB) as no-contract alternative.
- **Voice-centric** → every tariff has a German voice flat — size by data; L/XL only if calls to EU/CH/GB/TR matter.
- **Roaming / travel-heavy** → MagentaMobil L/XL for the larger annual LG 2/3 volume; Travel & Surf only beyond it. EU trips need no pass.
- **Broad usage** → size by data (typically M or L); check PlusKarte and MagentaEINS for household value.
- **Several devices / family** → PlusKarte, PlusKarte Data, Kids & Teens (not a "family pool").
- **18–27** → MagentaMobil Young (more data for the price) after age check.
